Beyond the Finish Line

Our Commitment to
Transgender Welfare

Across much of the world, transgender people face hardship that starts early in life and often follows them into adulthood. In many societies, especially where social acceptance and legal protections are still limited, a family that discovers a child is transgender may see it as a source of shame. Worried about how they will be seen in their community, some parents disown these children and force them out of the home, leaving them to fend for themselves at a young age.

Even children whose families accept them are not spared. At school, transgender children are often bullied, harassed and isolated by classmates, and sometimes by teachers. Facing this every day, many leave school early. They lose the education that could have led to stable work and independence. Not every society has the awareness, support systems or protections that would make schools and workplaces safe for them.

The consequences are severe. With no family support, no education and no job opportunities, many transgender people are pushed to the edges of society. Many end up begging on the streets, and many are forced into sex work or other risky and unlawful ways of surviving.

They did not choose this. It comes from being shut out at every stage of life.

Our pledge

At Veeran Vitality, we believe a business has a responsibility to the society it is part of. As a socially responsible organisation, we will give 10% of our profits to transgender welfare. The money will support efforts that help transgender people get an education, build a livelihood, and live with the dignity, safety and opportunity everyone deserves.
